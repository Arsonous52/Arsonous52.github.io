Flappy Bird

Overview

A faithful homebrew recreation of the classic Flappy Bird, built for the Nintendo 3DS. This port captures the original game's addictive gameplay on the 3DS. Developed from the ground up, it runs smoothly on hardware and is designed to feel native to the system. Simple, responsive, fun, and maybe just a little anger inducing.
